# Problem frame contract

Use a brief record for a consequential decision or a handoff. Keep unknown fields explicit and avoid invented precision.

- **Decision and initial claim:** What choice must be made, and what was first proposed?
- **Observed problem:** Affected actor, context, symptoms, sources, and relevant time window.
- **Desired outcome:** Consequence that matters and the source of any priorities or constraints.
- **Candidate explanations:** Evidence for, evidence against, confidence, and a fact that would change each explanation.
- **Boundaries:** Authorized scope, accountable decision owner, existing commitments, and unresolved authority.
- **Problem statement:** A solution-neutral description of the gap between observed and needed conditions.
- **Risks:** Material consequences of acting on the wrong explanation and of waiting.
- **Probe:** Hypothesis, comparison or baseline, affected population, observation window, signal, observer, and stopping condition.
- **Decision rule:** Existing accepted threshold and its source, or a clearly labeled proposed threshold awaiting the appropriate decision.
- **Handoff:** Next action, owner, unresolved questions, and evidence needed to revisit the frame.

Trace later proposals to the observed problem and desired outcome. A requested feature, completed task, or polished artifact is not evidence that the causal explanation is correct.
