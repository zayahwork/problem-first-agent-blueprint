# Outcome learning record

Keep the prediction alongside the observation. Use a separate row or brief section for each material claim so one kind of success cannot stand in for another.

- **Original decision:** Problem, hypothesis, expected outcome, source, and date or relative timing.
- **Test identity:** Artifact or workflow version, cohort, observation window, baseline or comparison, and relevant context changes.
- **Decision rule:** Exact prior threshold, source, and accepting owner when known. Mark new or retrospective thresholds as proposed.
- **Evidence quality:** Sources, freshness, coverage, sample, missing data, and measurement limitations.
- **Claims assessed:** Operational, adoption, demand, causal, and economic as relevant. For each, record expected result, observed result, evidence, classification, confidence, and practical limit.
- **Cost and cross-effects:** Measured and estimated costs, failures, side effects, and effects on established priorities.
- **Alternative explanations:** Confounders and what evidence would distinguish them.
- **Belief update:** What changed, strength of the update, scope of generalization, and preserved uncertainty.
- **Next decision:** Adopt, reverse, hold, or test; rationale, accountable owner, and execution authority.
- **Next observation:** Discriminating question, signal, responsible actor, review point, and stopping condition.
- **Reuse boundary:** Where the evidence and any generalized lesson may be retained or reused under existing instructions.

Classify claims as supported, contradicted, inconclusive, or invalid within the tested scope. Do not substitute completion for operational quality, operational quality for adoption, adoption for demand, correlation for causation, or gross benefit for net economic value.
