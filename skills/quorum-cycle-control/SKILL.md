# Quorum Cycle Control

Coordinate evidence about competing outcomes before changing a shared artifact. A quorum here is a structured review of material concerns, not a vote and not a new source of authority.

## Establish the decision

- State the governing problem, proposed action, existing outcome priorities, hard constraints, and accountable owner. Recover authority from the user's instructions and established delegation. A coordinator inherits only the authority already given to it.
- Identify the current artifact or system state and relevant inputs: requirements, evidence, environment, constraints, and owner priorities. Unknown inputs remain unknown.
- Identify the required participants and affected-outcome perspectives, synthesis owner, independent evidence verifier, and authorized decider. Set an evidence standard and verification scope proportionate to the risk; one person may cover compatible responsibilities, but the proposing agent's confidence is not independent verification.
- Set a finite review budget in time, rounds, or both, scaled to consequence, uncertainty, urgency, and cost. State what ends the review. A routine conflict may need one response round; a complex one may justify more within the bound.
- Keep the disputed shared artifact stable during review. Specialists provide proposals and evidence rather than repeatedly overwriting one another. Independent inspections or explicitly authorized isolated experiments can proceed.

## Compare and challenge

1. Give each specialist a bounded concern and common relevant evidence. Collect positions independently where useful.
2. For each proposal, record expected gains, losses, uncertainty, constraints, and effects on other important outcomes. Use a compact proposal-by-outcome comparison when it clarifies tradeoffs.
3. Resolve factual disagreements with evidence first. Expose value disagreements to the owner. Use qualitative priority relationships unless justified numerical weights already exist or the owner accepts proposed weights; do not invent a global score.
4. Invite focused responses to material assumptions or a compatible synthesis within the review budget. Preserve minority evidence even when several participants prefer another option.
5. Have the independent verifier check decision-relevant evidence against the stated standard and report pass, fail, or inconclusive within its scope. Agreement among models relying on the same source is not independent evidence.
6. Classify repeated transitions with the cycle test below. End repetitive exchanges and choose an evidence-gathering probe or route the unresolved decision to its owner.

## Detect actual cycles

Treat a reversal as a candidate cycle only when the relevant inputs and decision context are unchanged. Record the repeated state or recommendation, transition, input identity, and the concern driving each transition. Alternating scores alone are insufficient.

Exclude deliberate rollback, authorized recovery, temporary experiments with planned restoration, progress through distinct lifecycle states, and changes justified by new evidence or changed requirements. These may still need review, but are not evidence of nonconvergence by themselves. If input identity cannot be established, report a suspected cycle rather than asserting one.

## Decide or leave unresolved

Before selecting a candidate, confirm that required perspectives are represented, evidence verification is adequate for the risk, material objections have a recorded disposition, and the ruling is within existing authority. Only the accountable human may explicitly authorize participant substitution or accept a missing required perspective; record that authorization. Neither participant count, unanimity, silence, nor a timeout satisfies a missing gate or creates approval.

Choose within existing delegated authority using the established priorities and hard constraints. A candidate with an unresolved hard-boundary question cannot be marked selected; leave it deferred for evidence or decision, or reject it when the evidence supports rejection. Hard boundaries cannot be averaged away. Prefer a separately authorized reversible probe when it can resolve material uncertainty at acceptable cost.

On time or round exhaustion, record the best-supported options, remaining disagreement, and the accountable owner or unresolved ownership. Keep the disputed action pending unless an existing rule authorizes a fallback. Do not assume restoration to a previous baseline is safe or authorized; identify a valid recovery state and authority before recommending or executing recovery.

End with the decision or explicit unresolved status, rationale, dissent, authorized executor, observer, review point, and stop condition. Execution is a separate step under the user's existing scope. For a durable record, read [references/quorum-record.md](references/quorum-record.md). No other skill or service is required.
