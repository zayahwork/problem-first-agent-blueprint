# Quorum record

Use this record for a consequential conflict or repeated coordination problem.

- **Decision:** Governing problem, disputed action, current state identity, and accountable owner.
- **Authority:** Existing delegation, execution boundary, and any owner decision still needed.
- **Required coverage:** Required participants and affected-outcome perspectives, synthesis owner, independent evidence verifier, and authorized decider. Record missing coverage and the accountable human's explicit authorization for any substitution or acceptance of an absent perspective; silence does not count.
- **Priorities and constraints:** Established ordering and sources; distinguish owner-accepted criteria from proposals. Numeric weights are optional and require a defensible basis.
- **Relevant inputs:** Requirements, evidence, environment, and other facts that would materially change the decision.
- **Review bound:** Time or round limit, reason for its scale, stopping condition, and timeout route.
- **Positions:** Specialist concern, evidence, uncertainty, expected gains, losses, and cross-effects for each option.
- **Challenges:** Material rebuttals, evidence changes, and proposed syntheses; preserve unresolved minority evidence.
- **Verification:** Risk-proportionate evidence standard, independent checks, verifier identity, pass/fail/inconclusive result, and scope limits. Shared-source model agreement is not independent evidence.
- **Cycle assessment:** Repeated state or recommendation, transition sequence, and confirmation of unchanged relevant inputs. Explicitly check recovery, rollback, planned restoration, lifecycle transitions, and new evidence before classifying a cycle.
- **Sufficiency:** Confirm required perspective coverage, adequate verification, recorded disposition of material objections, and authority before selecting a candidate.
- **Result:** Decided, needs evidence, needs human decision, or stopped; mark each candidate selected, rejected, or deferred with reasons. A candidate with an unresolved hard-boundary question cannot be selected; it may be deferred or rejected. Preserve unresolved options and owner escalation without tallying votes as authority.
- **Follow-through:** Authorized executor, observer, review point, stop condition, and any verified recovery option with its authority.

The record demonstrates considered coordination, not correctness. A budget ending leaves unresolved matters unresolved unless a previously authorized decision rule applies.
